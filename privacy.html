Privacy Policy

Sorted: UK Tax Check does not collect, send or share any personal data.

The pay and tax figures you enter, and any calculations you save, are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Links to GOV.UK open in your web browser, where GOV.UK’s own privacy policy applies.

The app gives estimates only and is not connected to or approved by HM Revenue & Customs.
