Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Sorted: UK Tax Check (“the app”), made by Steven Williams.

Your tax figures

The pay, profit and tax figures you enter, and any calculations you save, are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to the developer, to Google, to RevenueCat or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

If you haven’t bought “Remove ads”, the app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads.

In the UK and EEA you’re asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with “Ad privacy settings” in the app. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking when asked; you can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google’s use of data is covered by its own privacy policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The one-off “Remove ads” purchase is handled by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to check whether you’ve bought it. RevenueCat receives your purchase history for this app and a random ID created by the app. It doesn’t receive your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Removing ads

After buying “Remove ads”, the app stops loading Google AdMob, so no advertising data is collected from that point.

Other

Links to GOV.UK open in your web browser, where GOV.UK’s own privacy policy applies.

The app gives estimates only and is not connected to or approved by HM Revenue & Customs.
